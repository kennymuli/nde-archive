==============================================================================
METHOD NOTE - why spontaneous rates are not from regex
==============================================================================
The regex lexicon was validated against 80 blind-labelled narratives
and retired: micro recall 0.40, precision 0.84, with per-element
recall from 0.00 to 0.93. Inter-labeller kappa was 0.96, so the
failure was the patterns, not the task. Worst offenders:
    border                 regex recall 0.00
    chose_return           regex recall 0.00
    peace                  regex recall 0.04
    life_review            regex recall 0.12
    reluctant_return       regex recall 0.17
    altered_time           regex recall 0.20
